# Grouping reports

Read this file before changing event boundaries. First establish [independent eligibility](eligibility.md#report-subject); grouping cannot make a background fragment eligible.

## Default boundaries

Use current `events` and their IDs as starting boundaries. Map retained IDs to their final bullets: putting multiple IDs in one bullet is a merge, even if each is labelled “retained” or separately attributed; putting one ID in several bullets is a split. Before changing a boundary, record the involved IDs and original passages establishing the shared or distinct substantive action. Without that evidence, preserve the starting boundary. ID coverage alone does not validate grouping. A merge warning calls for review, not automatic splitting; neither item count nor a shared product justifies combining IDs.

## Same action

Merge duplicate coverage when the canonical subject/object, concrete action, stage and defining scope agree. Prefer bound claims and their original spans over generated titles, summaries or keywords. Missing information proves neither equality nor conflict; check the original surrounding prose. If a shared action's defining identity remains unconfirmed, preserve the existing boundaries. Separately attributing similar results does not permit merging independently eligible reports or treating one as the other's supporting observation. A source's alternative headline angle or extra parameter does not by itself establish another action.

Shared Apple context, device family, source, date, broad topic, analyst name, number or causal relationship is insufficient. A comparison, historical mention, related link, compatibility list or model used as background cannot bridge unrelated reports. The same rule applies to weak/deferred sources.

## One report, several facts

A coherent announcement, rollout, code disclosure, test, field investigation, market dataset, joint procurement or organizational initiative can contain many products, observations, prices, locations, conditions and attributed positions. Preserve that reporting unit and assign its supporting facts within it. Separately developed experiments or investigations retain their own observer, method and sample even when one article covers both; assign its corresponding passages to each group. That shared article cannot bridge otherwise distinct reports. Fact decomposition for accurate attribution is not bullet decomposition.

For example, the queues, inventory, purchase limits, channel quotes and explanatory forecasts in a field report can describe the same investigated market situation. They do not each become a separate item because their verbs or speakers differ. If another source independently reports a policy change or forecast revision as its subject, apply the action test to that report; neither a shared URL nor different URLs decides the outcome.

Keep a source's joint release or explicit common operation together with its named products/features. In a genuine multi-topic news collection or roadmap, split only the independently developed subjects and actions, not incidental mentions, background generations or a speculative aside. A heading or an exclusive fact is insufficient. The same source URL can support several qualifying reports through their own passages.

## Distinct actions

Keep independently reported launches, later ordering/shipment starts, changes in price or supply, measurements, disclosures, responses and legal procedures distinct when their actual actions differ. A launch announcement's future preorder and delivery dates remain facts of that launch; they are not yet separate opening/shipment reports. Opposing positions within one joint court filing remain facts of the filing unless the source establishes separate procedural acts.

Preserve the defining scope:

| Family | Identity to check |
| --- | --- |
| Software releases | Platform/version, release channel, stage and explicitly shared release wave. A version must belong to the named software, not a price or incidental number. |
| Security | Bulletin/version or the concrete exploit, component and attack path. Shared security/CVE language is insufficient. |
| Legal/regulatory | Actual parties, procedure, affected object and jurisdiction. A response is not a verdict; a requested order is not an issued order. |
| Tests/markets | Observer or reporting institution, method/dataset, object, region/period and operation. Different values inside one dataset remain attributed facts; matching values do not identify a shared test or report. |
| Prices/availability | Official policy versus observed quote/transaction, channel, region, currency, model and actual change. Forecast, offer, observed trade and completed order are distinct roles. |
| Content/services | Named work/season or service object, action, and any defining territory/plan. Shared Apple TV/Music branding is not a shared action. |
| Hardware/supply | Product/component, design or procurement/production/availability action and stage. Supplier or device references used only as attribution/context do not define that action. |

## Narrow shared operations

- A source-established cross-platform beta/RC train of the same version and stage is one release wave. Public beta, developer beta, RC, internal testing and final releases retain their own stages; background references to earlier builds cannot replace the announced channel. Individual stable OS releases or security paths do not merge solely by date.
- The first publication and direct withdrawal, correction, restoration or replacement of the exact same first-party page/document/configuration may form one chronological lifecycle item. Preserve initial facts, changed status and all sources. This exception does not absorb later product launches, enforcement or independent developments.
- An explicitly joint product announcement, catalog withdrawal or service selection can cover several named objects. A media outlet's list of unrelated launches is not itself a joint Apple operation. Separate works/actions stay separate unless sources establish that common operation.
- Localized coverage of the same official campaign can merge when its name/asset and occurrence agree. Adjacent dates need actual timezone evidence; an activity at the event, attendee plan, production report or survey is not the campaign itself.

After a merge or split, retain every material fact under the source that supports it. Do not duplicate the same proposition in two items merely to give a review fragment a destination. Check boundaries across the completed brief, including across the two categories.

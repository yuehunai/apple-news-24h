# Eligibility and categories

Use this file for new bullets, deferred promotion, exclusion or category changes. Binding a claim, deciding eligibility, grouping reports and choosing a category are distinct decisions.

## Report subject

A standalone item must be the actual subject of a factual report, established by its title, opening and substantive coverage, or a separately developed news item within a genuine news collection. A title alone can be misleading; a heading, local attribution, present tense, number, named object or action verb alone is not sufficient either.

Incidental related-news sentences, background references and non-theme passages in commentary, tutorials or weak third-party articles may supplement another independently eligible report. They cannot create a new item. If no eligible report needs them, exclude them with a reason. Do not turn a source's facts into separate news merely because they can be bound to different verbs or categories. A focused short news report can qualify; there is no minimum sentence count, length or number of sources.

Evaluate the source's reporting act, not merely the product or event it discusses. A factual statement used to frame an opinion, preview or recap does not turn that framing information into a report of its own. Establish eligibility from the source's substantive coverage before selecting clauses. A report of a release, field investigation or test remains one reporting unit with its material details unless the source establishes additional independently eligible subjects. See [grouping](grouping.md) when that boundary is disputed.

## Eligible actions

Include current factual reporting about Apple-controlled products, operating systems, firmware, built-in apps, services and content; research, development, manufacturing, supply, retail and after-sales operations; corporate decisions, personnel, facilities and transactions; and legal, regulatory or security actions directly involving Apple. Apple need not announce or perform the action: a reported defect, abuse or failure of its own product or service can qualify before Apple responds.

Use the reliable article publication time and the run's fixed window. An ongoing action, attributed plan, current responsibility, support condition or observed behavior can predate the window. Preserve its actual date and stage, without presenting it as a new launch. Excluding a recap requires evidence that the report merely repeats old material; read beyond a historical opening for a current disclosure or clarification.

Keep reported plans, forecasts, code discoveries, beta changes and commitments at their stated stage. Single-source coverage, uncertainty words or disagreement between sources does not justify automatic exclusion or correction. A claim that something is not planned, supported or observed can itself be the current report. Do not infer implementation, causation or confirmation from adjacent facts.

## Special boundaries

- **First-party functionality and support:** dedicated reporting of what a current capability does, where it works, its operating conditions, fees or limits qualifies without a new rollout, new discovery or malfunction. Apply this before the tutorial exclusion: how-to wording and instructions do not disqualify a report whose subject is the capability or a concrete operating observation. Omit click paths and advice while retaining behavior and necessary conditions. Exclude general walkthroughs or preference advice without that reporting subject; an incidental functional observation in an opinion article cannot establish one.
- **Measurement and investigation:** reporting focused on an actual test or investigation of Apple hardware, its operation or a concrete deployment can qualify with a method/configuration and observed results. An individual, open-source tool, first-person voice, qualitative result or recommendation in the same article is not disqualifying. Preserve the tested setup, baseline, limits and complete relevant series. Apply the experience-review exclusion to general product appraisals, not reporting focused on one specific operation with stated conditions and observed results. Specifications, isolated measurements or first-hand comments inside a general appraisal do not change its reporting subject. Competitor-led comparisons using Apple as a reference, proposed tests, theoretical specifications and independent modification showcases do not qualify through this rule.
- **Markets and pricing:** attributable current Apple sales, shipments, market share, rankings, financial or stock results require a reporting party and a defined market/period/object. A qualitative directional observation can qualify too. A reported price movement needs detail review even when its headline also mentions a subsidy. Distinguish the reported sample from a third-party retailer's published offer or discount table; media republication, multiple models or a low price does not convert an offer into a market observation. Satisfaction polls and purchase intent are not measured sales. Forecasts remain forecasts.
- **Ecosystem:** concrete new or expanded interoperability between an Apple platform/device and another platform/device can qualify, including through partner software or an existing protocol. So can a concrete connection to a first-party Apple data platform or its API, such as reading, importing or synchronizing that data. New OS support, a patcher, driver, system adaptation or hardware acceleration on Apple hardware can also qualify, including experimental/virtualized support. Independently porting Apple software to non-Apple hardware as a modification demonstration does not become platform-support news merely by using first-party software. Ordinary third-party app availability, release-channel changes or adaptation to existing system interfaces/display modes do not establish that milestone; neither do accessory compatibility, configuring an existing VM or integrations only between third-party services.
- **Institutional and company actions:** an attributable current purchase/deployment or Apple decision/role can qualify. Existing device use, a personal usage story or a third party's asset disposal does not become an Apple action. Distinguish Apple personnel acting in their actual responsibility from outside commentary about Apple.
- **Legal and safety:** identify the affected party, object, jurisdiction and actual procedure or incident. Apple devices used as evidence in an unrelated case are background. A party's filing, requested relief and the court's decision are different stages; keep allegations and responses attributed.
- **Services and media:** Apple production, publication, catalog, availability or other concrete service/content actions can qualify. A work merely playable on Apple devices, a critic's viewing recommendation or unrelated sporting result does not. Relevant participants, results and history may support a retained service action without becoming separate items.

## Excluded forms

Exclude routine retail promotions and affiliate pitches, purchase advice, opinion or wish lists, historical/rumor recaps without current reporting, ordinary independent apps/accessories, competitor marketing and independent hardware modification/repair projects. Judge the source's role, not a keyword alone. A current factual report can contain detachable advice; conversely, facts in a weak article do not automatically change its reporting focus.

Keep a directly reported speaker's statement, forecast or technical explanation when material to an eligible report. Exclude the secondary writer's added inference, calculations, motives, significance, consequences and advice, even if presented with attribution or in a headline. Exclude the whole inferred proposition, not just words such as “means” or “therefore.” Preserve adjacent independent observations when they belong to the retained report.

Same-action rewrites can add source coverage to a retained report; do not discard their URLs merely because their facts repeat another source. Reprints do not make a recalled past action newly occur, or turn commentary into an eligible report.

## Categories

Classify the actual object and operation, not its host device or a background name:

| Category | Primary objects and operations |
| --- | --- |
| `software_systems` / 软件与系统 | OS, firmware, apps, interfaces, authentication and other software behavior; platform interoperability, developer rules, online services/content, billing/support policy and legal/regulatory actions. |
| `hardware_products` / 硬件与产品 | Physical products/components, design and production, hardware measurements/defects, physical repair, supply and market activity, ordering/delivery and physical retail/facility operations. |

Company/personnel reports follow the stated role and operation. A capability running on a phone is not hardware merely because the phone is in the headline; a physical component discovered in code is not necessarily a software release. Preserve the report's actual disclosure/action. A different category alone is not a reason to split an established combined action.

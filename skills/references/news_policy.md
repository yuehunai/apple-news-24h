# Apple 24-Hour News Policy

This index preserves the policy entry point. The four files below own separate decisions; read the needed file directly from the skill workflow, not all four for every run.

| Task | Authoritative reference |
| --- | --- |
| Decide whether a report stands alone, review a deferred item, or choose its category | [Eligibility](eligibility.md) |
| Decide whether reports share one action or need distinct bullets | [Grouping](grouping.md) |
| Resolve evidence, preserve facts and write/check the final brief | [Evidence and writing](evidence-writing.md) |
| Investigate source coverage, publication time or collection failure | [Collection](collection.md) |

Source-local fact binding and review flags do not establish independent news eligibility. Implementation details and regression examples belong to the crawler and tests, not additional product instructions.

# Apple 24-Hour News Brief

A bare `$apple-news-24h` requests a Chinese brief for the preceding 24 hours. The crawler already collects, groups and classifies the evidence. Consume those results and resolve their review items rather than repeating the whole pipeline manually.

The detailed [policy](references/news_policy.md) remains authoritative. Consult its relevant section for an unresolved boundary or collection failure; ordinary runs do not require reading its entire implementation-rule catalogue.

## 1. Collect once

Resolve the script relative to this skill directory and choose an output for this run:

```bash
python3 scripts/apple_news_24h.py --hours 24 --timezone auto --format json --output latest.json
```

Use the requested IANA timezone when one is specified. Wait for actual process completion, then read the JSON file; stdout is a status record and `*.brief.md` is a coverage scaffold, not the finished brief. Do not interrupt merely because collection takes longer than expected.

A completed zero-event or suspiciously sparse first result requires the **same command** rerun with network approval. If that rerun remains sparse, add `--include-diagnostics` and inspect source/fetch failures and discovery/detail counts. Healthy sources may legitimately yield little news. For unavailable sources use the policy's [source list](references/news_policy.md#sources) and [discovery/fallback rules](references/news_policy.md#candidate-discovery); do not fill gaps with weak items or old news.

If the crawler is unavailable, follow the policy’s manual fallback and record source URLs, publication times, actions and material facts; do not fabricate crawler JSON.

The managed cache is cleared at startup and contains only diagnostic evidence. Never reuse an older JSON or cached page as this run's freshness evidence.

## 2. Use the structured result

| Input | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| `events` | These have passed the crawler’s policy decisions. Preserve their ID boundaries, tiers, categories and source ownership. Reopen a decision only for a specific policy conflict demonstrated by captured evidence, or a `merge_warning` requiring review. A warning alone does not justify merging or exclusion. Before splitting a retained event, record the captured passage that establishes a different substantive action or stage; another attribution, extra parameters or publication order alone is insufficient. |
| `summary`, `key_facts`, `must_include_facts`, `fact_sources` | Read these together: material facts may appear only in `summary`. Preserve the named actor, attribution, quantities and conditions. `origins` identifies the supporting source or exact span. `owned` marks binding to a projected claim's span, not Apple ownership or eligibility; `false` is not an exclusion reason. |
| `final_brief_queue` | Account for every required entry. For `unprocessed_evidence`, decide whether each material fragment completes an existing action, supports a separate eligible report, or is excluded by policy. In mixed sources, distinguish a local factual report from adjacent commentary; the parent’s roundup or opinion tone does not exclude a concrete local report. |
| `source_evidence` | Follow `evidence_id` to read the relevant unit and surrounding context when a fragment, pronoun, attribution or boundary needs clarification. Reopen a live page only if the captured evidence is insufficient. A queue entry with `capture_status: discovery-only` contains an excerpt, not verified full text: check the source for material missing claims when relevant. If unavailable, use only supported captured facts and record the unresolved gap; the marker does not promote the item. |
| `deferred_events` | Exclude weak items as independent bullets, but decide their required fragments separately. A fragment may support, complete or qualify a retained action; keep its source URL with that action. Before promoting a separate item, identify the captured passage and resolve the weak reason under the eligibility rules below. The report may concern a concrete release, change or disclosure, or independently focus on current first-party functionality, support or operating conditions. Existing capabilities used only to explain another story or give usage advice remain context. A concrete product, future date or named reporter alone does not establish freshness. |

Do not repeat full-article review or settled event decisions. Apparent implausibility, lack of independent corroboration, single-source coverage, differing versions/figures and a shared topic do not justify overruling an event. Preserve attribution and uncertainty. Review warnings and unassigned material: a queue fragment's `required` flag requires a decision, not automatic publication.

`must_include_facts` requires coverage of each distinct material fact and qualifier, not verbatim reproduction of source sentences. Before building the fact checklist, separate factual content from the article writer's added inferences about motives, consequences or significance. A number, attribution or inclusion in this field does not make such an inference mandatory. Judge each proposition, not the writer's tone or the passage as a whole: reported attributes, measurements, actions and operating conditions do not become editorial inference merely because they appear beside commentary. Then check relevance separately: sharing a company or person, or appearing in a fact field, is not enough. Keep facts that describe or explain the action's specific content, matters involved, reported results, operating conditions or necessary background. If a detail's only connection to the action depends on an excluded author inference, exclude it too and record why. Supporting facts need not themselves be new; preserve material existing capabilities and conditions that explain the action's use or scope. This does not exclude a named forecast, disclosure, party response or official explanation that is itself the selected news, or material background needed to understand it; retain the claim, attribution and uncertainty. Do not omit an otherwise material fact because it lacks its own new Apple action; other omissions require a demonstrated source or ownership error. The scaffold and title list do not override demonstrated crawler errors.

## 3. Resolve only the boundaries that need a decision

- **Time:** use the reliable source publication time and the JSON's fixed window. Older dates do not exclude newly reported evidence or material background. Distinguish a source's current report from an explicit recap: an attributed report of a current plan, responsibility or observed behavior need not prove that no earlier article reported it. Preserve its attribution and stage. Explicitly recalled prior plans do not become new actions merely through republication; inspect the following passage for a current disclosure or clarification before excluding the report. A newly specified event format can qualify even when the date and products were already reported. Dedicated current functionality and support reports follow the Guides rule below; the feature's original launch date is not their eligibility test.
- **Ownership:** a source may discuss Apple, competitors and history together. Keep each statement with its actual actor. A comparison method or a quoted opinion is not Apple's implementation or an official finding.
- **Supporting detail:** event eligibility does not have to be proved again for every fact. Preserve material details of the same named work, broadcast, incident or measurement, including its participants, results and explanatory history. They need not change Apple's product or subscription terms, and do not become separate events merely because another actor performed them.
- **Measurements:** a newly reported quantitative test of Apple hardware can qualify without an Apple announcement. An independent developer, open-source tool or custom setup does not by itself make the measured result a personal-project showcase. Preserve the tested configuration, method, numeric series and limits; exclude subjective upgrade advice or a project with no material Apple-device finding.
- **Prices:** distinguish a newly reported Apple-device regional price series from a retailer's sale or coupon. Observed reseller/user prices can qualify with their attribution and conditions; do not call them official Apple prices or a new policy. Remove incidental buying advice without discarding the price report.
- **Ecosystem:** a concrete rollout of Apple platform interoperability may qualify even when a partner announces it. A new OS-patcher release adding system support for Apple hardware also qualifies; it need not be Apple's own release or a hardware measurement. Ordinary third-party app compatibility, accessory promotion and competitor comparison remain weak. Content merely available on an Apple platform does not establish Apple production, publication or a new catalog action; a story about existing device use does not establish a new institutional deployment.
- **Stages:** current beta changes, commitments and newly reported code clues can qualify; preserve “testing”, “planned” or “not enabled”. Do not exclude solely because a feature has not shipped.
- **Merging:** compare each action as actor + concrete verb + object. A coordinated release or disclosure may have several constituent features or products: keep them together when the sources establish one coordinated rollout or disclosure batch, with compatible release versions, and preserve each constituent’s facts and conditions. Different device names or operating-system surfaces alone do not require separate bullets. Separate distinct substantive actions, explicitly successive disclosures and changed stages; a partnership or product family alone does not establish a shared batch. For reports about a product configuration or change, the named informant is claim attribution, not a separate product action. Keep compatible accounts of the same subject and concrete change together, with attribution attached to each claim; their publishers or informants need not have made a joint announcement. Extra parameters alone do not establish another disclosure. Missing counterparties, dates or list members likewise do not establish a separate action; preserve those qualifiers only for the claims whose sources state them. A retained event reviewing several features in the same app and version does not need splitting merely because each feature has a different verb or object; a split still requires a separately established action or stage. Keep implementation clues for one feature together when they share the same attributed code disclosure, platform/version and main component; different clues alone do not prove separate actions. A court ruling and a party's response or appeal stay separate even in the same case; a feature and a provider's response also stay separate. Shared topic, URL, OS version or background alone does not prove duplication.
- **Guides and conflicting reports:** a dedicated report of current first-party functionality, support coverage, cost or operating conditions qualifies even when the capability debuted earlier and no new rollout occurs in this window. Preserve current status and limits without presenting them as a new launch. Tutorials, usage advice and other stories that only refer to established capabilities remain weak. Judge the report's actual subject; a version name or “added” in supporting text alone is insufficient. Recommendation wording or first-person observations alone do not make dedicated factual coverage a tutorial: distinguish a body reporting capabilities and conditions from one mainly giving instructions or opinions. Single-source coverage or differing versions/figures calls for attribution, not unsupported correction or deletion.

Use the policy's [inclusion rules](references/news_policy.md#inclusion-rules), [tiers](references/news_policy.md#relevance-tiers) or [merge rules](references/news_policy.md#event-level-merge-rules) for a boundary this summary does not resolve. Do not invent a new rule to shorten the brief.

## 4. Draft and check in small batches

You may scan the event and queue index first. Then finish one to three actions per batch; a single multi-action source may exceed three so its fragments stay together. For each batch: read its summary, structured facts and required fragments; write a short fact checklist naming each member of material lists and numeric series, then draft the paragraphs using the sentence rules below; check both fact coverage and sentence necessity before marking a paragraph checked. Then move on. Do not deeply read all fact material first and then draft everything from memory. Assemble the checked paragraphs without shortening them again. The checklist can be plain notes; no special script or additional framework is needed.

Preserve complete relevant numeric series and lists, per-model/region conditions, dates, eligibility, prices, official responses, uncertainty and operational limits. Relevance comes before completeness: a historical release appendix is not a current support matrix or upgrade requirement. Record its exclusion in the notes; keep an explicitly relevant prior build or baseline with the current change. Keep quantities attached to their units and conditions: a per-order limit is not a per-person limit. Do not replace a supported detail with a vague summary, borrow a fact from a different actor, or infer a cause from a measurement. There is no target sentence count: one sentence is enough for a small change; detailed reports need as many as their material facts require.

Write news facts, not commentary on the brief:

- State the action and its material details directly. Keep selection, exclusion, splitting, merging and category decisions in the notes, even when those decisions are correct. Do not explain in the bullet why it qualifies or how it differs from another bullet.
- Attach attribution and uncertainty to the claim they qualify: “据 X 报道，苹果计划……” or “X 在测试版代码中发现……”. Do not repeat them at the end as “这些仍属传闻” or “这是该媒体的判断”. Preserve a distinct sourced limitation or denial, such as “功能尚未启用”, “是否兼容现有设备尚不明确” or “展示图为 AI 模拟图”; these are facts, not filler.
- Before marking the paragraph checked, inspect its last sentence **and trailing clauses**, then any similar wording elsewhere. If removing a passage loses no distinct material fact, attribution or necessary condition, remove it; moving it earlier is not a fix. For example, after reporting a quota change, omit “这是另一项限额调整”; after stating a change and linking both sources, omit “两家媒体均记录了这项变化”. Do not add generic significance claims, the writer's extra inferences about motives or consequences, or reader advice anywhere in the paragraph. Apply the fact-versus-interpretation distinction above before the sentence-necessity check.

Keep dispositions in the same compact notes. For a retained source, name any material fragment excluded and its specific reason; “same source”, “background” or a linked URL alone does not account for unwritten facts. Factual comparisons may stay attributed within an eligible action without becoming separate third-party news. Before returning the brief, verify:

1. Every current event ID and required queue entry has a supported disposition: a bullet, a proven same-action duplicate, or a specific exclusion. Multi-action fragments need individual decisions; “reviewed” alone is not evidence of coverage.
2. Each retained action's material facts and qualifiers appear in the actual paragraph. Matching IDs or URLs does not prove this.
3. Bind each bullet's URLs to its own facts and action using `origins` or captured evidence. When splitting an event into smaller actions, distribute URLs by support; do not copy the event's full source list into every bullet. Retain all supporting URLs, including multiple URLs from one publisher and repeated URLs supporting independent actions. A source need not support every claim in a bullet: keep it when it supports part of that action, with the attribution attached to its claims. Record unrelated fragments, canonical duplicates and excluded actions in the notes.

## 5. Return the brief itself

Return the checked paragraphs verbatim; do not make a shorter second version for the final response. Output only `**软件与系统**` followed by `**硬件与产品**`, with one eligible action per bullet by default. Use readable Chinese and put all source links at the end of each bullet: `（来源：[MacRumors](...), [9to5Mac](...)）`.

End each bullet after its checked news facts and source links. Do not add a wrap-up or review explanation during final assembly.

For a truly empty category write `在指定时间窗口内，该分类下没有发现符合条件的新闻。` Do not append status, diagnostics, review explanations, cache notes or a link in place of the brief. Saving a draft or reporting that checks passed does not complete the invocation.

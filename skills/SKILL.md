# Apple 24-Hour News Brief

A bare `$apple-news-24h` requests a Chinese brief for the preceding 24 hours. The crawler already collects, groups and classifies the evidence. Consume those results and resolve their review items rather than repeating the whole pipeline manually.

The deliverable is the complete brief in the final response. Saving it to a file does not deliver it; do not substitute a completion report or file link.

The detailed [policy](references/news_policy.md) remains authoritative. Consult its relevant section for an unresolved boundary or collection failure; ordinary runs do not require reading its entire implementation-rule catalogue.

## 1. Collect once

Resolve the script relative to this skill directory and choose an output for this run:

```bash
python3 scripts/apple_news_24h.py --hours 24 --timezone auto --format json --output latest.json
```

Use the requested IANA timezone when one is specified. Wait for actual process completion, then read the JSON file; stdout is a status record and `*.brief.md` is a coverage scaffold, not the finished brief. Do not interrupt merely because collection takes longer than expected.

A completed zero-event or suspiciously sparse first result requires the **same command** rerun with network approval. If that rerun remains sparse, add `--include-diagnostics` and inspect source/fetch failures and discovery/detail counts. Healthy sources may legitimately yield little news. For unavailable sources use the policy's [source list](references/news_policy.md#sources) and [discovery/fallback rules](references/news_policy.md#candidate-discovery); do not fill gaps with weak items or old news.

Check `collection_warnings` even when the total event count is healthy: it marks unresolved source-window coverage. Use the same source's discovery/fallback rules to check that gap, and record any remaining limitation in the notes without treating an empty listing response as proof of no news.

If the crawler is unavailable, follow the policy’s manual fallback and record source URLs, publication times, actions and material facts; do not fabricate crawler JSON.

The managed cache is cleared at startup and contains only diagnostic evidence. Never reuse an older JSON or cached page as this run's freshness evidence.

Before splitting a retained event, record the captured passage that establishes a different substantive action or stage; another attribution, a separate article's focus, extra parameters or publication order alone is insufficient. |
| `summary`, `key_facts`, `must_include_facts`, `fact_sources` | Read these together: material facts may appear only in `summary`. When a review fragment supports a separate action, determine its eligibility and category from its own actor and operation under the policy; it does not inherit the parent event's tier or category. Facts that complete an existing action stay with that action.

Do not repeat full-article review or settled event decisions. Apparent implausibility, lack of independent corroboration, single-source coverage, differing versions/figures and a shared topic do not justify overruling an event. Preserve attribution and uncertainty. Review warnings and unassigned material: a queue fragment's `required` flag requires a decision, not automatic publication.

`must_include_facts` requires each distinct material proposition and qualifier, not verbatim sentences. The single evidence-to-draft workflow in section 4 applies to summaries, facts and required review fragments alike.

## 3. Resolve only the boundaries that need a decision

- **Time:** use the reliable source publication time and the JSON's fixed window, not the date the reported action began. An otherwise eligible current report of an ongoing action, plan, responsibility or observed behavior remains eligible when its action date predates the window. Preserve that date, attribution and stage; do not rewrite it as a new launch. Excluding a recap requires evidence of recap framing, not merely an older action date or the possibility of earlier coverage. Explicitly recalled prior plans do not become new actions merely through republication; inspect the following passage for a current disclosure or clarification before excluding the report. A newly specified event format can qualify even when the date and products were already reported. Dedicated current functionality and support reports follow the Guides rule below; the feature's original launch date is not their eligibility test.
- **Ownership:** bind each statement to its actor, model and stage in the surrounding prose. Resolve pronouns from that prose; an image caption identifies the pictured object and does not by itself change the subject of neighboring claims. A source covering several models or release channels does not make each fact apply to all of them. Keep a build, restriction or change with the model and stage named in its own passage; add another source’s detail under that source’s attribution. Before excluding a condition as belonging elsewhere, identify the wording that establishes that boundary. A comparison method or a quoted opinion is not Apple's implementation or an official finding.
- **Supporting detail:** event eligibility does not have to be proved again for every fact. Preserve material details of the same named work, broadcast, incident or measurement, including its participants, results and explanatory history. They need not change Apple's product or subscription terms, and do not become separate events merely because another actor performed them.
- **Measurements:** a newly reported quantitative Apple-hardware test with its method or configuration and measured results can qualify whether performed by an individual, reviewer or institution. Apple hardware or its tested configuration must be the report’s measurement subject; a competitor-led benchmark using Apple only as a reference remains weak. First-person wording, a review headline or an upgrade verdict does not cancel that measured finding. Preserve its configuration, method, numeric series and actual measurement limits. Omit proposed future tests, generic technical scenarios and arguments about conclusions readers should avoid, as well as purchase advice, subjective impressions and unrelated specifications. An open-source tool or custom setup does not by itself make the result a project showcase. Keep comparison periods, per-model figures, ranks and baselines within one reported dataset together; different periods separate independent reports, not facts in that series.
- **Prices:** distinguish a newly reported Apple-device regional price series from a retailer's sale or coupon. Observed reseller/user prices can qualify with their attribution and conditions; do not call them official Apple prices or a new policy. Remove incidental buying advice without discarding the price report.
- **Ecosystem:** a concrete rollout of Apple platform interoperability may qualify even when a partner announces it. A new OS-patcher, driver or system adaptation that enables OS support or hardware acceleration on Apple hardware also qualifies, including experimental or virtualized support. Merely configuring an existing VM or running a third-party app does not establish new platform support. Ordinary app compatibility, accessory promotion and competitor comparison remain weak. Content merely available on an Apple platform does not establish Apple production, publication or a new catalog action; a story about existing device use does not establish a new institutional deployment.
- **Stages:** current beta changes, commitments and newly reported code clues can qualify. Keep the source’s stage on each claim: an announced commitment remains “said it would” or “planned” unless the source establishes implementation. Do not infer implementation from neighboring current features or from absence of contrary evidence. Preserve “testing” or “not enabled”; lack of shipment alone does not disqualify a report.
- **Merging event IDs:** keep separate IDs unless their source titles and opening leads establish the same actor, concrete action, object, stage and defining scope, such as the reported dataset or regional table, subject to the policy’s narrow claim/document lifecycle exceptions. Preserving differing qualifiers and attribution within a combined paragraph does not establish that shared identity. State that action narrowly; “product update”, “roadmap” or “sales schedule” is a topic, not proof of duplication. A source-established distinct price forecast, configuration disclosure, launch, pre-order opening or shipment retains its action even when reports repeat prices, specifications or dates. Re-reporting the ordering or delivery schedule already established by a launch supplies launch details; a separate headline does not establish a new stage. An independently occurring order opening or delivery start retains its stage. Shared article, newsletter issue, informant, publication date, presentation or background likewise does not establish a shared action. An aggregate source may support several separately bound events without joining them. Keep each URL with the actions its passages support.
- **Keeping an established action together:** one documented release, rollout or substantive operation may cover several features, products or OS surfaces. A platform, component or capability described only as part of a device disclosure belongs with that device unless the source establishes a separately focused action. A distinct name, subsection heading or software/hardware category does not establish one. Keep constituent facts, compatible versions, prices, specifications and dates together when they support that action. Keep a coordinated procurement or order-adjustment batch's per-model increases and decreases together; a different model or direction within that batch is not a new action. Funding, implementation and reported progress may be facets of one substantive initiative update. Extra parameters, different informants or missing counterparties, dates or list members alone do not establish separate actions; preserve each claim's own attribution and qualifiers. “Said” or “posted” attributes a claim rather than creating a new action, and an executive's restatement or explanation need not become another event. A split requires evidence of a different substantive action, initiative, commitment or stage: for example, a court ruling and a party's response or appeal stay separate. If the relation to a referenced announcement determines the boundary, inspect it before changing the group. A retained report reviewing several features in one app/version may stay together, as may implementation clues for one feature from the same attributed code disclosure, platform/version and main component. Apply this rule within an established action; do not use it to override the preceding test for merging distinct IDs.
- **Guides and conflicting reports:** a dedicated report of current first-party functionality, support coverage, cost or operating conditions qualifies even when the capability debuted earlier and no new rollout occurs in this window. Qualification under this rule establishes eligibility, not a separate event boundary; apply the preceding action-boundary rules to decide whether the report stands alone or supplies facts to an established event. Preserve current status and limits without presenting them as a new launch. Tutorials, usage advice and other stories that only refer to established capabilities remain weak. Judge the report's actual subject; a version name or “added” in supporting text alone is insufficient. Recommendation wording or first-person observations alone do not make dedicated factual coverage a tutorial: distinguish a body reporting capabilities and conditions from one mainly giving instructions or opinions. Single-source coverage or differing versions/figures calls for attribution, not unsupported correction or deletion.

These action boundaries also apply within a single source: distinguish a reported dataset from separate forecast revisions or observed market actions. A shared research note does not by itself make them one action. Distinct propositions require independent eligibility under the policy before becoming separate bullets; different metrics, attribution or wording alone do not establish another action.

Use the policy's [inclusion rules](references/news_policy.md#inclusion-rules), [tiers](references/news_policy.md#relevance-tiers) or [merge rules](references/news_policy.md#event-level-merge-rules) for a boundary this summary does not resolve. Do not invent a new rule to shorten the brief.

## 4. Complete each action before moving on

Scan the event and queue index, then finish one to three actions at a time. Gather the action's summary, structured facts and relevant review fragments, including deferred sources, before drafting. Read a source once when it supports several actions and assign its passages accordingly.

Use the crawler's facts as the starting checklist; add short notes for unresolved or missing material propositions. Do not copy whole articles or drafted sentences into a new checklist. No special ledger format, second checklist or validation script is needed.

### Establish the propositions from the evidence

For each material proposition, keep its source location and **actual subject + action + object or specific operation**, with attribution and conditions. A feature name is not its operation. Resolve pronouns from their original clauses before combining sentences; several nearby participants do not thereby perform the same action. Expand “both”, “either” and “all” into the affected members and operations in the notes.

Decide each proposition separately:

- **Retain** direct reports, observations, attributed plans, forecasts, responses and explanations relevant to this action. They need neither a second named source nor repetition in the body. “Possible”, “reportedly” or “rumor” describes certainty, not whether the statement is a fact report or the writer's deduction. Preserve that distinction and attribution.
- **Exclude first** the secondary writer's estimates or conclusions derived from reported facts or specifications, including added calculations, motives, significance, consequences and advice about what readers should infer. Attribution, calling it a forecast or placing it in a headline does not exempt the writer's deduction. Keep the reported baseline or observation in a mixed passage; excluding one sentence's interpretation does not exclude its neighbor's concrete operation.
- **Keep conflicts visible.** Record each source's incompatible date, value, description, direction or condition as a separate proposition. One source's uncertainty does not cancel another's affirmative report. Preserve both with their own attribution, including a substantive denial; do not select one or collapse them into generic uncertainty.
- **Check material relevance.** Supporting facts need not be newly disclosed or establish another Apple action, but must explain this action, work, incident, configuration or condition. Numbers, attribution and required flags do not make unrelated appendices or background mandatory. Record a concrete source, ownership or relevance reason for each omitted material fragment; do not reject a mixed paragraph under one “commentary” label.

For fragments flagged for attribution review, identify the wording that establishes the speaker before retaining an attributed prediction. Do not transfer a writer's prediction to a quoted source or a current-model observation to a future device.

### Draft and verify against those propositions

Draft from the evidence checklist, then map each retained proposition to its short expression in the paragraph. A true duplicate may use the same expression only when object, operation and conditions agree. A drafted paragraph copied into notes cannot prove coverage.

Preserve complete relevant lists and numeric series, attributes, units, per-model/region conditions, dates, prices, eligibility, official responses and operational limits. A name or umbrella label does not cover distinct outputs or input methods. State baseline attributes that define a configuration or comparison; “unchanged” alone is insufficient. Keep severity, thresholds and degree with the capability they qualify. Distinguish reaching a multiple of a baseline from increasing by that multiple.

Omit click paths and API syntax; retain settings, setup states and other prerequisites that determine whether or where a capability works, even in developer instructions. Historical release appendices are not current support matrices, but a relevant prior build or baseline may qualify the current change. Keep each condition with its own operation, model, version and source; separate clauses when only one listed capability has a prerequisite. Name the device containing a component rather than using an ambiguous pronoun.

Compare in both directions: every retained proposition must appear, and every factual clause must be supported by its own passage. Check actual actors and operations as well as numbers. Do not infer causes from measurements or silently resolve contradictory sources. There is no target sentence count; use the length the material facts require.

### Remove commentary before closing the action

Write the action and facts directly. Selection, exclusion, splitting, merging and category explanations belong in notes. Attribution and uncertainty belong beside the claim, not in a repeated ending such as “这些仍属传闻” or “这是该媒体的判断”. Retain distinct sourced facts such as a feature not yet enabled, an unresolved compatibility limit or an image identified as a simulation.

Express each proposition once. Inspect the last sentence **and trailing clauses**, then the rest of the paragraph, for repeated reporting status or credibility commentary. For example, “据 X 估计，可能达到 Y” already states the status; omit “这是预测，并非已实现结果”, wherever it appears. Keep a source's specific denial, confirmation status, missing field or operational limit when it adds a distinct fact. Report what was measured and its actual scope, without advice about broader conclusions readers should avoid. Remove generic significance, motives and consequences; moving them earlier does not fix them.

Only after these checks mark the action and its queue fragments complete. Assemble checked paragraphs without shortening them again.

Before returning:

1. Every current event ID and required queue entry must have a supported disposition: a bullet, a demonstrated same-action duplicate or a specific exclusion. Multi-action fragments need individual decisions; “reviewed”, a shared URL or a copied source paragraph proves none of them.
2. Bind each bullet's URLs to the passages supporting its own action. Distribute sources when splitting; never copy a mixed event's entire URL list into every child. Retain every supporting URL, including repeated publishers, duplicate coverage and one source supporting multiple independent actions. A source need support only part of a bullet, with attribution kept on its claims. Record unrelated fragments, canonical duplicates and excluded actions in the notes.

## 5. Return the brief itself

Your final message to the caller must contain the checked paragraphs verbatim, even when another agent invoked the skill. Do not hand off delivery through a completion report, file link or instruction to use a saved draft. Output only `**软件与系统**` followed by `**硬件与产品**`, with one eligible action per bullet by default. Use readable Chinese and put all source links at the end of each bullet: `（来源：[MacRumors](...), [9to5Mac](...)）`.

End each bullet after its checked news facts and source links. Do not add a wrap-up or review explanation during final assembly.

For a truly empty category write `在指定时间窗口内，该分类下没有发现符合条件的新闻。` Do not append status, diagnostics, review explanations or cache notes.
